The Contrarian Thesis

The breach isn't technical. It's human.

Enterprises spend fortunes hardening networks while the real door stays open: a person who can be deceived, recruited, or walked past.

Espionage crews, hostile activists, and malicious insiders don't brute-force encryption. They exploit trust, access, and process. Compliance checklists never see them coming.

We operate the way the adversary would. Deliberately, patiently, without permission to fail. Then we hand you the exact blueprint of how they get in, and the countermeasures that shut it down.
