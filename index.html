Our Security Services

Corporate protection services that find how your company could be robbed of its data, property, and reputation, and stop it.

Red Cell Solutions is a corporate physical security and counterintelligence consulting firm serving clients nationwide from our South Florida base. We protect companies from the adversaries that cost them money and reputation: competitors collecting trade secrets, insiders walking out with data, and bad actors targeting your facilities, and we focus on the layer most security programs ignore: your people, your buildings, and the everyday procedures attackers exploit.

A firewall cannot stop an intruder who walks through your front door. Our physical penetration testing services put your locks, badge readers, cameras, and front-desk procedures to the test with realistic, controlled intrusion attempts. Organizations that need to measure their entire security posture against a determined adversary turn to our red team operations, which combine physical intrusion, social engineering, and open-source intelligence into a single coordinated exercise.

Testing is only half the job. Our corporate counterintelligence services turn findings into a program that defends your trade secrets and reputation, and our insider threat programs address the hardest problem of all: the people you already trust. For a documented baseline, a physical security assessment and audit reviews your facilities, policies, and protection measures, and our security awareness training programs teach your employees to recognize and report the tactics real adversaries use.

That work runs across every facility type we serve. Our manufacturing plant security engagements trace how trade secrets physically leave a plant, and our data center security consulting validates the layered access control that SOC 2 and ISO 27001 auditors expect to see working. If you want a baseline before you talk to anyone, our physical security assessment guide and printable physical security checklist show you exactly what to look for.

We Test, Then We Plan

Our methodology: prove what an adversary could take first, then build the program that stops them.

Adversarial Testing

We test your defenses the way a competitor, insider, or intruder would, under documented rules of engagement, and prove exactly what could be taken.

Counterintelligence Program Design

We turn findings into a program: access discipline, visitor handling, sensitive-area controls, and procedures built around your specific risks.

Implementation & Training

We help your team put the plan in place and train your employees to recognize and report the tactics real adversaries use.

Every engagement follows this sequence because lasting protection requires more than a report. Testing proves where your company is exposed today, not where a checklist says it might be. The plan turns that evidence into a counterintelligence program your organization can actually run. Implementation and training make sure it holds up when a real adversary tries it.
