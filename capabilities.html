Capabilities

Penetration testing, social engineering& insider threat defense.

Red Cell Solutions tests your people, buildings, and processes the way a real attacker would, then helps you fix what we find. Here is how we help companies stop break-ins, scams, and insider theft.

Service 01

Physical Penetration Testing & Red Teaming

Our team physically breaks into your buildings the way a real intruder would, then shows you exactly how they did it. A physical penetration test puts your locks, badges, cameras, guards, and employees to a live test.

  • ▸ Covert building and facility entry testing.
  • ▸ Badge cloning and access-control bypass.
  • ▸ Tailgating, impersonation, and guard bypass.
  • ▸ Server room and data center penetration.
  • ▸ Photo evidence, attack timeline, and fixes.

Service 02

Social Engineering Testing: Phishing, Vishing & Pretexting

Most breaches start with a person, not a computer. We test how your employees respond to phishing emails, phone scams, and in-person impersonation, then train them to catch the next attempt.

  • ▸ Phishing and spear-phishing email testing.
  • ▸ Phone-based (vishing) and text-message scams.
  • ▸ In-person impersonation and pretexting.
  • ▸ Executive and high-value-target protection.
  • ▸ Security awareness training for your team.
Request social engineering testing

Insider Threat & Corporate Espionage Defense
