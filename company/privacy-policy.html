Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information on redcellsolutions.com

Effective date: July 14, 2026

Red Cell Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") provides physical security consulting services from Coral Springs, FL. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect through this website, redcellsolutions.com, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to the practices described here.

Information You Provide to Us

You can browse this website without providing any personal information. We collect information you choose to submit, including:

  • Contact form submissions: your name, email address, phone number, organization, and the contents of your message.
  • Security qualification assessment submissions: your contact details and your answers to the assessment questions.
  • Direct communications: information you share when you call, email, or otherwise contact us.

Form submissions are delivered to our business email and stored in our internal customer records, which are hosted on Google services.

Information Collected Automatically

Website Visitor Identification (Apollo.io)

This website uses a visitor tracking technology provided by Apollo.io, a business to business data platform. When you visit our pages, this technology collects information such as your IP address, device and browser characteristics, and the pages you view. Apollo may match this information against its business contact database to identify the company you work for and, in some cases, your business contact profile. We use this information to understand which organizations are interested in our services and for business development outreach. You can learn more in the Apollo.io Privacy Policy.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Apollo.io tracker described above uses cookies or similar technologies to recognize visitors across pages and sessions. We do not use our own advertising cookies, and we do not run third party advertising networks on this site.

Hosting and Server Logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub may collect visitor IP addresses in its server logs for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To identify organizations that may benefit from our services and conduct business to business outreach.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it with data brokers for their own use. We share information only with service providers that support our business operations:

  • Apollo.io: receives website visitor data as described above. Depending on your state of residence, this type of disclosure may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under applicable privacy laws, and you have the right to opt out as described below.
  • Google: processes form submissions and hosts our business email and customer records.
  • GitHub: hosts this website and its server logs.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or a valid governmental request, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of Red Cell Solutions or others.

Your Privacy Choices and How to Opt Out

You can limit or opt out of the tracking described in this policy in the following ways:

  • Contact us directly: email contact@redcellsolutions.com with the subject line "Privacy Request" to ask us to delete the information we hold about you or to opt out of future outreach. You can also call 954-496-0657.
  • Opt out with Apollo.io: Apollo maintains its own opt out and data removal process for individuals in its database. You can submit a request through the Apollo.io Privacy Center.
  • Browser controls: tracking protection features, content blockers, and cookie controls in your browser can prevent the visitor identification script from loading.

This website is a static site and does not currently respond to browser based opt out signals such as Global Privacy Control. Please use the methods above instead.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights regarding your personal information, including the right to know what we have collected, the right to access or correct it, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of sharing for targeted advertising. Residents of California and a number of other U.S. states have such rights under their state privacy laws. We honor these requests from all visitors regardless of location. To exercise any of these rights, email contact@redcellsolutions.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 45 days and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our services are directed to businesses in the United States. Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you may have additional rights, including the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Contact us at the address above to exercise these rights.

Data Retention

We keep inquiry and client records for as long as needed to serve you, operate our business, and meet legal and professional obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or render it anonymous.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold, including access controls on our business systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected on this page before they take effect.
