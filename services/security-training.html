Workplace Violence Prevention Training

Prepare your employees to recognize, report, and respond to threats inside the workplace

Security awareness does not stop at phishing and tailgating. Workplace violence prevention training addresses one of the most serious risks your employees can face: threatening or violent behavior from coworkers, customers, or outsiders. Like our other programs, it is built around your organization's specific environment, roles, and risk profile rather than a generic slide deck.

Training focuses on the skills that matter when minutes count:

  • Recognizing warning signs and concerning behavior before an incident occurs
  • Reporting procedures that get concerns to the right people quickly
  • Responding safely during an active incident, including when and how to evacuate, shelter, or summon help
  • Understanding your organization's workplace violence prevention plan and each employee's role in it

For many employers this is now a legal obligation, not just good practice. California Senate Bill 553 (SB 553) requires most California employers to establish a workplace violence prevention plan and train employees on it, effective July 2024, and other jurisdictions are moving in the same direction. Learn more about our full workplace violence prevention training program, or contact us to discuss a program scoped to your workforce.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about security awareness and workplace violence prevention training

Why is security awareness training important?

Security awareness training is important because the human element is involved in 74% of data breaches, per the IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report 2024. Technology alone cannot stop social engineering; employees who recognize phishing, pretexting, and tailgating become an active layer of defense instead of the easiest target.

How often should security awareness training be conducted?

Security awareness training works best as a continuous program rather than a one-time event. Effective programs combine periodic formal sessions with regular reinforcement, knowledge checks, and simulated exercises. Benchmark data shows phishing susceptibility continues to fall across 12 months of sustained, continuous training.

How effective is security awareness training?

Sustained training measurably reduces risk. The KnowBe4 2025 Phishing Benchmark Report found an 86% reduction in phishing susceptibility after 12 months of continuous training, and the 2024 benchmark showed average employee susceptibility falling from 34.3% to 4.6% after one year of sustained awareness training.

What is workplace violence prevention training?

Workplace violence prevention training teaches employees to recognize warning signs, report concerning behavior, and respond safely during an incident. In California, SB 553 requires most employers to adopt a workplace violence prevention plan and train employees on it, effective July 2024, making structured training a compliance requirement as well as a safety measure.
