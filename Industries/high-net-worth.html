What Does a High Net Worth Security Consultant Do?

Independent advice on protecting your family, residences, and family office

Advice on Your Side of the Table

A high net worth security consultant assesses how well your residences, family office, and daily routines are protected, then designs the program and standards that fix what falls short. Red Cell Solutions is a consulting firm, not a guard company: we do not sell staffing, equipment, or monitoring contracts, so our recommendations answer to your interests alone.

That independence matters in a market where much of the advice available to wealthy families comes from companies selling the very services they recommend. Through our physical security consulting practice, we tell you what your situation actually requires, what it does not, and how to hold every vendor you hire to a written standard.

  • Assessment first: Understand your actual exposure before spending on cameras, gates, or personnel
  • Program design: Written plans and procedures that family members and household staff can realistically follow
  • Vendor independence: We help you evaluate protection and technology vendors, and we take nothing from any of them
  • Discretion throughout: Engagements handled quietly, with information limited to the people who need it

What Does an Estate Security Assessment Cover?

A layered review of the property, the systems, and the people who run the household

Every Layer of the Property

An estate security assessment covers the property from the street to the interior: perimeter and grounds, entry points, camera and alarm coverage, lighting, and the household procedures that determine whether the technology actually gets used. The deliverable is a written estate security plan that ranks findings by risk and sequences improvements sensibly.

Our residential assessments follow the same disciplined method as our commercial physical security audits, adapted to the realities of a private home: family routines, guests, deliveries, contractors, and staff who need to work without feeling watched.

  • Perimeter: Fencing, gates, landscaping sightlines, and the approaches a trespasser would actually use
  • Access: Vehicle gates, pedestrian entries, door and lock hardware, and how keys, codes, and credentials are issued and revoked
  • Camera and alarm strategy: Whether coverage, detection zones, and alarm response match the property, not just the installer's proposal
  • Household staff procedures: Screening, key control, visitor and delivery handling, and what staff should do when something feels wrong
  • Daily patterns: How predictable routines, publicly visible property details, and social media exposure affect the family's privacy

Which Consulting Services Do We Offer Private Clients?

Assessment, program design, and independent evaluation, never staffing

Residential and Estate Security Assessments

A structured evaluation of one residence or an entire portfolio of properties, covering perimeter, access, technology, and household procedures, with findings ranked by risk in a written report.

Estate Security Planning

Design of the complete protection plan for a property: perimeter strategy, access control approach, camera and alarm system strategy, lighting, and written procedures for family members and household staff.

Family Office Security Programs

Security policies and procedures for the office that manages the family's affairs: physical access control, visitor handling, employee screening and offboarding, protection of sensitive documents, and verification procedures for unusual requests.

Executive Protection Program Design and Vendor Evaluation

When protective staffing is warranted, we define the requirement, write the standards for licensing, insurance, training, and conduct, evaluate candidate firms, and help you compare proposals on substance. We design and oversee the program; the vendor you select provides the personnel.

Travel Security Planning

Advisory planning for personal and business travel: pre-travel risk review for destinations, itinerary and accommodation considerations, communication plans, and sensible guidance for family members who travel frequently.

Adversarial Testing of Existing Security

For families who already have mature security in place, an authorized red team engagement tests how the estate's defenses, technology, and staff procedures hold up against a realistic, controlled adversary, before a real one tries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about estate security, family office security, and executive protection consulting

Does Red Cell Solutions provide bodyguards or armed protection agents?

No. Red Cell Solutions is a security consulting firm. We assess residences and estates, design security programs, and help families define requirements, evaluate the firms that provide protective staffing, and hold those vendors to clear standards. Keeping consulting separate from staffing means our recommendations are never shaped by a payroll we are trying to fill.

What is estate security?

Estate security is the layered protection of a private residence and the people who live and work there. It combines the physical perimeter, controlled entry points, camera and alarm coverage, lighting, and the daily procedures followed by family members and household staff. A strong estate security plan coordinates all of these layers so no single failure leaves the property exposed.

What is a family office security program?

A family office security program is the set of written policies and procedures that protect the principals, staff, offices, and sensitive information a family office manages. It typically covers office access control, visitor handling, employee screening and departures, protection of financial and personal information in physical form, travel procedures, and how staff verify unusual requests before acting on them.

How do I choose an executive protection company?

Start by defining your requirements before speaking with any vendor: the concerns driving the decision, the coverage you actually need, and the standards for licensing, insurance, training, and conduct a firm must meet. Then evaluate candidates against those written requirements. An independent consultant helps you set the requirements, question vendors knowledgeably, and compare proposals on substance rather than presentation.

How is confidentiality handled during an engagement?

Discreetly, from first contact onward. Red Cell Solutions limits engagement details to the people who need them, communicates through the channels the client chooses, and is glad to work under a confidentiality agreement. Reports and findings belong to the client, and we do not publicize client identities or engagements.
