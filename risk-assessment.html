Understanding Your Vulnerabilities

Our Social Engineering Risk Assessment provides a thorough evaluation of how vulnerable your organization is to various social engineering tactics. We identify where your human firewall has gaps and provide actionable recommendations to strengthen your defenses.

Our expert team uses a combination of technical analysis, behavioral science, and industry best practices to deliver insights that matter.

  • Identify vulnerable departments and individuals
  • Evaluate current security awareness and training effectiveness
  • Analyze past incidents and near-misses
  • Review policies and procedures for gaps
  • Assess physical security controls
Learn About Our Process

Our Assessment Process

A methodical approach to understanding and mitigating social engineering risks

1

Discovery & Analysis

We begin by gathering information about your organization, including structure, culture, and existing security controls. We review documentation, interview key stakeholders, and analyze past security incidents.

2

Testing & Evaluation

Our team conducts simulated social engineering tests tailored to your industry and organization. These may include phishing simulations, vishing calls, pretext scenarios, and physical security tests.

3

Reporting & Recommendations

We deliver a comprehensive report detailing findings, risk levels, and prioritized recommendations. This includes specific action items to address vulnerabilities and strengthen your human firewall.
