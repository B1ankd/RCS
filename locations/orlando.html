Your Orlando Facility Through an Adversary's Eyes

Orlando's economy runs on large campuses, large workforces, and valuable information: hospitality operations with thousands of badges in circulation, simulation and technology firms holding contract-sensitive research, healthcare systems handling regulated patient data, and data centers holding everything their customers store. Every one of those environments can be tested the way an adversary would test it, and most never have been.

Red Cell Solutions brings authorized adversarial testing and corporate counterintelligence consulting to Central Florida companies. We prove whether an outsider can talk or tailgate their way into your facility, whether an insider could walk out with files, devices, or product, and whether anyone would notice, and then we design the program that closes what we found. We sell no guards, hardware, or monitoring, so our findings are not a sales pitch.

  • Physical penetration testing of entry points, badge systems, and front-desk procedures
  • Insider threat and data exfiltration testing for facilities that handle sensitive information
  • Documented security assessments with prioritized, budget-aware remediation
  • Counterintelligence program design: access discipline, visitor handling, sensitive-area controls

Do You Serve Orlando from South Florida?

Yes: on-site work across Central Florida from a single Coral Springs base

Yes. Red Cell Solutions is based in Coral Springs, Florida, and performs on-site work in Orlando and across Central Florida from that single location. We do not maintain an Orlando office, and we would rather say so plainly than imply a local branch that does not exist. Site visits, penetration tests, and facility walkthroughs are scheduled as on-site trips; program design, document review, and reporting proceed remotely between visits, which is how consulting work naturally divides.

For most engagements this structure costs nothing in quality. A counterintelligence program is designed on paper and proven under retest, not by proximity, and an independent consultant two hundred miles away is worth more than a conflicted one around the corner. When an engagement calls for frequent physical presence, we say that too, and build it into the plan.

Which Orlando Organizations Do We Work With?

Central Florida's economy brings several distinct risk profiles

The region's venues, attractions, and hospitality operations employ large workforces on complex sites, which multiplies both insider risk and the stakes of prevention: our workplace violence prevention consulting covers threat assessment procedures, reporting channels, and response planning that stand up to real staffing numbers, and our insider threat programs address what thousands of badges in circulation actually mean for your data and property.

The simulation and technology corridor around research park and UCF concentrates contract-sensitive research and intellectual property that competitors would pay to see: exactly the exposure our technology industry practice and counterintelligence program design are built for. Orlando's healthcare systems and data centers face regulated-data and physical exfiltration risk every shift.

Sustained growth also keeps construction active across the metro, and our construction site security assessments address the equipment theft, material shrinkage, and after-hours exposure that follow active job sites anywhere. And Central Florida's congregations face the same tension as congregations everywhere, staying open while protecting people: our house of worship security consulting builds greeter protocols, access plans, and emergency procedures that volunteer teams can actually sustain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about physical security consulting in Orlando

Do you serve Orlando from South Florida?

Yes. Red Cell Solutions operates from a single location in Coral Springs, Florida, and travels to Orlando and Central Florida for on-site work. Assessments, penetration tests, and facility walkthroughs are conducted in person; program design, reporting, and follow-up proceed remotely between visits. What does a physical security assessment in Orlando cover?

A documented review of your facility's physical controls and the procedures around them: entry points and locks, badge and visitor management, camera coverage, reception and delivery handling, and how sensitive areas and information are protected from intrusion, insider theft, and data exfiltration. Findings arrive as a prioritized report your team can act on immediately.

Do you provide security guards in Orlando?

No. Red Cell Solutions is a consulting firm, not a guard or staffing agency. We test and assess your security, design the program that fixes what we find, and if staffing is genuinely warranted, we write the standard any vendor you hire should meet. Because we sell no guards, hardware, or monitoring, our advice answers to you alone.

Do you conduct investigations?

No. We are security consultants, not a private investigative agency. Our work is preventive: authorized adversarial testing, security assessments, and counterintelligence program design that stop data theft, espionage, and intrusion before they happen.

Which Orlando industries do you work with?

Our Orlando work follows the shape of the Central Florida economy: hospitality and attraction companies with large workforces and complex campuses, the simulation and technology corridor around research park and UCF, healthcare systems handling regulated patient data, data centers, active construction sites, and congregations. Each brings a different mix of espionage, insider, and intrusion risk.
