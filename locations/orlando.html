Executive Protection Consulting in Orlando, Florida

Red Cell Solutions is not a bodyguard or staffing agency. We design executive protection programs for Orlando principals, help you specify and select the right protection vendor, plan travel security, and assess residences, with advice that answers to you alone.

What Is Executive Protection Consulting?

The layer of independent judgment that sits above any staffing decision

Design and Oversight, Not Manpower

Executive protection consulting is the design and oversight of a protection program, as distinct from supplying the agents who staff it. Red Cell Solutions reviews your actual exposure, decides with you what coverage is genuinely warranted, writes the standards any protective staffing vendor must meet, and then helps you select and hold accountable the firm that provides the personnel.

Most executive protection searches in Orlando return staffing companies whose answer to every question is more agents, because agents are what they sell. Our executive protection and private client consulting takes nothing from any vendor, so we can tell you honestly when less coverage is enough, when a proposal falls short of your written standard, and when the money is better spent on the residence, the office, or the procedures around the principal. The same independence runs through our broader physical security consulting practice.

  • Exposure review before any staffing decision is made
  • Program design: coverage model, procedures, and written vendor standards
  • Vendor evaluation, selection support, and contract standards you own
  • Ongoing advisory oversight once the program is running

How Do We Help Orlando Clients Build a Protection Program?

Four stages, each producing a document you keep and control

1. Define the Requirement

We start with the concerns driving the decision and a review of the principal's actual exposure: residence, office, routines, travel, and public visibility. The output is a written requirement stating what coverage is warranted and, just as important, what is not.

2. Write the Vendor Standard

Before any Orlando staffing firm is contacted, we document the standards a vendor must meet: Florida licensing, insurance, training and experience, conduct expectations, reporting requirements, and how performance will be measured under the contract.

3. Evaluate and Select

We help you identify candidate firms, question them knowledgeably, and score proposals against your written standard rather than their presentation. You choose the vendor; our role is to make that choice an informed one.

4. Oversee and Adjust

Once the program runs, we review performance against the standard, advise on adjustments as circumstances change, and add advisory travel security planning and residential security assessments as the principal's needs evolve.

Do You Serve Orlando from South Florida?

Yes: on-site work across Central Florida from a single Coral Springs base

Yes. Red Cell Solutions is based in Coral Springs, Florida, and performs on-site work in Orlando and across Central Florida from that single location. We do not maintain an Orlando office, and we would rather say so plainly than imply a local branch that does not exist. Site visits, residential assessments, and facility walkthroughs are scheduled as on-site trips; program design, vendor evaluation, document review, and reporting proceed remotely between visits, which is how consulting work naturally divides.

For most engagements this structure costs nothing in quality. A protection program is designed on paper and proven in review meetings, not by proximity, and an independent consultant two hundred miles away is worth more than a conflicted one around the corner. When an engagement calls for frequent physical presence, we say that too, and help you build the requirement into the vendor standard so the staffing firm you hire carries it.

Which Orlando Organizations Do We Work With?

Executive protection is one thread; Central Florida organizations bring several others

Beyond principals and their families, our Orlando work follows the shape of the Central Florida economy. The region's venues, attractions, and hospitality operations employ large workforces on complex sites, and that scale makes prevention programs matter: our workplace violence prevention consulting covers threat assessment procedures, reporting channels, and response planning that stand up to real staffing numbers.

Sustained growth also keeps construction active across the metro, and our construction site security assessments address the equipment theft, material shrinkage, and after-hours exposure that follow active job sites anywhere. And Central Florida's congregations face the same tension as congregations everywhere, staying open while protecting people: our house of worship security consulting builds greeter protocols, access plans, and emergency procedures that volunteer teams can actually sustain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about executive protection consulting in Orlando

Do you provide bodyguards in Orlando?

No. Red Cell Solutions is a security consulting firm, not a bodyguard or staffing agency. We design executive protection programs, write the requirements a protective staffing vendor must meet, evaluate and help you select among candidate firms, and oversee the program once it is running. The agents themselves come from the licensed vendor you hire.

Does Red Cell Solutions have an Orlando office?

No. The firm operates from a single location in Coral Springs, Florida, and travels to Orlando and Central Florida for on-site work. Assessments and site visits are conducted in person; program design, vendor evaluation, and reporting can be handled remotely or on-site as the engagement requires.

How do I choose an executive protection company in Orlando?

Define your requirements in writing before speaking with any vendor: the concerns driving the decision, the coverage you actually need, and the standards for licensing, insurance, training, and conduct a firm must meet. Then score every Orlando-area candidate against that document. An independent consultant helps you set the requirements, question vendors knowledgeably, and compare proposals on substance rather than presentation.

What is travel security planning?

Travel security planning is advisory preparation for personal and business travel: a pre-travel risk review of destinations, itinerary and accommodation considerations, communication and check-in plans, and practical guidance for executives and family members who travel frequently. It is planning and advice, not an escort service.

Do you offer private security services in Orlando?

Not in the staffing sense. If you are searching for private security in Orlando because you need guards, we do not supply them; what we provide is the assessment that tells you whether you actually need staffing, the written standard any guard firm you hire should meet, and independent help selecting and overseeing that firm.

Get Independent Advice Before You Hire Anyone

Before signing with an Orlando protection vendor, talk to a consultant with nothing to sell but the answer. We will help you define the requirement, set the standard, and choose well.
