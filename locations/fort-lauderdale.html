What Does an On-Site Assessment in Fort Lauderdale Include?

First document what is in place, then, if you choose, test how it holds up

Audit First, Then Adversarial Testing

An on-site assessment starts with a structured audit: entry points, badge and key control, camera coverage, alarm procedures, visitor management, and the daily habits of the people who work in the building, each scored against defined criteria. You receive severity-rated findings, photographs and evidence, and a remediation roadmap ordered by risk.

When you want proof rather than paperwork, a physical penetration test takes the next step: with written authorization, we attempt to gain entry to your Fort Lauderdale facility the way a real intruder would, through tailgating, pretexting, and social engineering, then show you exactly where the attempt succeeded and why. For Broward County clients, the follow-up visit to verify fixes is easy to schedule, which is where much of the long-term value sits.
