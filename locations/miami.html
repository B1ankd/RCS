Physical Security Consulting in Miami, Florida

Independent security audits, penetration testing, executive protection program design, and vendor evaluation for Miami businesses and residences. Red Cell Solutions serves all of Miami-Dade County on-site from its South Florida base.

What Does a Physical Security Consultant Do for Miami Businesses?

Independent assessment and program design, with no guards or equipment to sell

Advice That Is Not a Sales Pitch

A physical security consultant assesses how well your Miami facility is actually protected, then designs the controls, procedures, and vendor standards that close the gaps. Many searches for private security in Miami lead to companies that sell guards, cameras, or monitoring contracts. Red Cell Solutions sells none of those things. Our physical security consulting practice delivers analysis and design only, so every recommendation answers to your interests rather than a product line.

Engagements typically begin with a structured physical security audit: a checkpoint-driven evaluation of access control, perimeter, camera coverage, visitor management, and personnel practices, scored against defined criteria and delivered with a prioritized remediation roadmap.

  • Structured audits with documented scoring criteria and severity-rated findings
  • Program design: written policies and procedures your staff can actually follow
  • Independent vendor evaluation for guard firms, integrators, and monitoring providers
  • On-site work anywhere in Miami-Dade County, from downtown and Brickell to Doral

Which Miami Industries Do We Work With?

The sectors that define the Miami economy each carry distinct physical security exposures

Financial Services and Banking

Miami is a major financial center, and banks, investment firms, and family offices concentrate valuable information behind their doors. Our financial services security consulting covers branch and office access control, visitor handling, and the personnel practices regulators expect to see documented.

Healthcare

Hospitals and clinics across Miami-Dade balance open public access with the duty to protect patients, staff, and medications. Our healthcare security assessments address emergency department exposure, pharmacy and supply security, and HIPAA-relevant physical safeguards.

Data Centers

South Florida's connectivity to Latin America makes the Miami area a data center and colocation market. Our data center security consulting evaluates layered access control, mantrap and cage procedures, and the audit evidence customers and auditors ask for.

High Net Worth Residences

For private clients in Miami Beach, Coral Gables, and the surrounding waterfront communities, we conduct estate security assessments covering perimeter, access, camera and alarm strategy, and household staff procedures, handled with strict discretion.

Cannabis Dispensaries

Dispensaries operating in Miami-Dade face cash handling exposure, product diversion risk, and state security requirements. We assess storefront layout, secure storage, transport procedures, and compliance documentation.

Do You Offer Executive Protection in Miami?

We design and oversee protection programs; vetted vendors provide the personnel

Program Design, Not Staffing

Yes, as consultants: Red Cell Solutions designs executive protection programs for Miami principals but does not supply bodyguards or armed agents. We define the actual requirement, write the standards a protective staffing vendor must meet for licensing, insurance, training, and conduct, evaluate candidate firms, and help you compare proposals on substance rather than presentation.

That separation matters. When the firm advising you also sells the staffing, the advice tends to expand the payroll. Because we take nothing from any vendor, our executive protection program design and vendor evaluation work can honestly tell you when less coverage is enough, and when a proposal does not meet the standard you set.

  • Threat and exposure review before any staffing decision is made
  • Written vendor requirements and evaluation criteria you own
  • Side-by-side proposal comparison and selection support
  • Ongoing program oversight and travel security planning on an advisory basis

How Should You Choose Among Security Companies in Miami?

Define the requirement first, then make vendors compete against it

Choose a Miami security company by writing down your requirements before you speak to any vendor, then scoring every proposal against that document. The Miami market offers many guard firms, integrators, and monitoring providers, and most buyers evaluate them on price and presentation because they have nothing better to measure against. An independent assessment changes that.

A typical engagement runs in three stages. First, a scored security audit establishes what your facility actually needs, separating genuine gaps from vendor upsell. Second, we translate those findings into written specifications: post orders for guard coverage, performance standards for camera and access control systems, and response expectations for monitoring. Third, we help you evaluate bids, question vendors knowledgeably, and structure contracts so performance is measurable. The result is that the security companies you hire in Miami work to your standard, not their template.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about security consulting in Miami

Does Red Cell Solutions have an office in Miami?

No. Red Cell Solutions is based in Coral Springs, Florida, in Broward County, and performs on-site work throughout Miami-Dade County from that single location. Audits, penetration tests, and assessments are conducted at your Miami facility in person, and reporting and follow-up can be handled on-site or remotely, whichever you prefer.

Do you provide bodyguards in Miami?

No. Red Cell Solutions is a consulting firm, not a protective staffing agency. For clients who need executive protection in Miami, we design the program, write the requirements and standards a vendor must meet, evaluate candidate firms, and help you oversee the vendor you select. The protective personnel come from the staffing firm you hire, not from us.

What areas of Miami do you serve?

All of Miami-Dade County, including downtown Miami, Brickell, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral, and the surrounding municipalities. Because every engagement begins with scoping, distance within the county has no effect on the depth of the assessment you receive.

How is a security consultant different from a Miami security company?

Most security companies in Miami sell guards, cameras, or monitoring contracts, so their advice tends to lead back to what they sell. A security consultant sells only analysis and design: an assessment of your actual exposure, a written program to address it, and independent help selecting and holding accountable the vendors who supply staffing or equipment.

How does an engagement in Miami start?

With a scoping conversation. We discuss the facilities or residences involved, the concerns driving the request, and any compliance obligations, then propose a defined scope of work with deliverables and a timeline before any on-site activity begins.
