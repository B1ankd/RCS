Security Consulting in West Palm Beach and Palm Beach County

Security audits, penetration testing, insider threat programs, and independent security consulting for the companies, congregations, schools, and professional offices of Palm Beach County. On-site service from our South Florida base.

What Does a Security Consultant Do in West Palm Beach?

Independent assessment and program design for properties and organizations across the county

Assessment Before Spending

A security consultant in West Palm Beach evaluates how well a company or organization is actually protected, then designs the program that fixes what falls short, before anyone spends money on cameras, gates, or personnel. Red Cell Solutions is a consulting firm only: we sell no staffing, equipment, or monitoring contracts, so our recommendations are shaped by your exposure rather than a product catalog.

Our physical security consulting practice serves Palm Beach County on-site from our Coral Springs base, one county south. Commercial and institutional work typically begins with a scored physical security audit; where sensitive data or intellectual property is at stake, authorized penetration testing proves whether the controls actually hold.

  • Evidence-based assessments with findings ranked by risk
  • Written programs that staff and volunteers can realistically follow
  • Independent evaluation of guard firms, integrators, and monitoring vendors
  • Discretion as a standing rule, not a special request

How Do We Serve Palm Beach County Companies?

Wealth management, healthcare, and professional firms concentrate here, and so does what adversaries want from them

Advice on Your Side of the Table

We serve Palm Beach County companies with security audits, authorized penetration testing, and corporate counterintelligence program design, never staffing. The county's concentration of financial firms, medical practices, and professional offices means businesses are courted constantly by companies selling guards, technology, and monitoring; our role is to sit on your side of the table, define what your situation actually requires, and hold every vendor to a written standard.

The exposure that matters most here is informational: client files, account data, and deal documents that leave with a departing employee or a convincing visitor cost more than any stolen equipment. Our insider threat programs test whether data can physically walk out of your office, then build the access lifecycle, offboarding, and escalation procedures that stop it.

  • Scored security audits of offices, from reception to records rooms
  • Authorized penetration testing and data exfiltration simulations
  • Insider threat program design: access, visitor handling, screening, verification procedures
  • Independent vendor evaluation for guard firms, integrators, and monitoring providers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about security consulting in West Palm Beach and Palm Beach County

Do you provide security guards in Palm Beach?

No. Red Cell Solutions is a consulting firm, not a staffing agency. We assess your security, design the program that fixes what we find, and if staffing is genuinely warranted, write the standards any guard vendor must meet and help you evaluate candidate firms. We supply no personnel, which keeps our advice independent of any payroll.

Does Red Cell Solutions travel to West Palm Beach?

Yes. The firm is based in Coral Springs, in neighboring Broward County, and performs on-site work throughout Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach, the Town of Palm Beach, and Boca Raton. There is no local branch office; assessments are conducted in person from the single South Florida location.

What does a physical security assessment in Palm Beach County cover?

A physical security assessment covers your facility from the street inward: perimeter and sightlines, vehicle and pedestrian entries, camera and alarm coverage, lighting, badge and visitor management, and the procedures your staff actually follow. Findings are ranked by risk in a written security plan, and engagements are handled with strict confidentiality.

Do you work with houses of worship in Palm Beach County?

Yes. We help Palm Beach County congregations protect services, religious schools, and childcare areas while staying welcoming: greeter and usher protocols, access control for weekday operations, and emergency procedures designed for organizations that run largely on volunteers.

Do you assess private schools in Palm Beach County?

Yes. School assessments cover campus perimeter and entry control, visitor screening, dismissal and carline procedures, and staff readiness, with findings written so administrators and boards can act on them in priority order.
