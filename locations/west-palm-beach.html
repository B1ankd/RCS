Security Consulting in West Palm Beach and Palm Beach County

Estate security assessments, executive protection program design, and independent security consulting for the families, congregations, schools, and professional offices of Palm Beach County. On-site service from our South Florida base.

What Does a Security Consultant Do in West Palm Beach?

Independent assessment and program design for properties and organizations across the county

Assessment Before Spending

A security consultant in West Palm Beach evaluates how well a property or organization is actually protected, then designs the program that fixes what falls short, before anyone spends money on cameras, gates, or personnel. Red Cell Solutions is a consulting firm only: we sell no staffing, equipment, or monitoring contracts, so our recommendations are shaped by your exposure rather than a product catalog.

Our physical security consulting practice serves Palm Beach County on-site from our Coral Springs base, one county south. Commercial and institutional work typically begins with a scored physical security audit; private residential work follows the same discipline, adapted to the realities of a household.

  • Evidence-based assessments with findings ranked by risk
  • Written programs that families, staff, and volunteers can realistically follow
  • Independent evaluation of guard firms, integrators, and monitoring vendors
  • Discretion as a standing rule, not a special request

How Do We Serve Palm Beach Estates and Family Offices?

Private wealth concentrates here, and so does the need for independent security advice

Advice on Your Side of the Table

We serve Palm Beach County's private clients with estate security assessments, family office security programs, and executive protection program design, never staffing. Palm Beach's concentration of private wealth means residents are courted constantly by companies selling guards, technology, and monitoring; our role is to sit on your side of the table, define what your situation actually requires, and hold every vendor to a written standard.

For principals considering protective coverage, the phrase executive protection usually means hiring agents. Our version is the step before and above that: a review of your actual exposure, written requirements for licensing, insurance, training, and conduct, evaluation of candidate firms, and oversight once a vendor is engaged. Advisory travel security planning rounds out the program for families who move frequently between properties.

  • Residential and estate security assessments, single properties or portfolios
  • Family office security programs: access, visitor handling, screening, verification procedures
  • Executive protection program design and independent vendor evaluation
  • Advisory travel security planning for principals and family members

Which Palm Beach County Organizations Do We Work With?

Beyond private clients, county institutions face their own distinct exposures

Houses of Worship

Palm Beach County congregations of every faith balance openness with protection. Our house of worship security consulting designs greeter and usher protocols, plans for services and holiday crowds, and safeguards for religious schools and childcare, built for volunteer-run organizations.

Schools

Private and independent schools across the county carry a duty of care that parents scrutinize closely. Our school security assessments cover campus perimeter, entry and visitor control, dismissal procedures, and staff readiness, delivered in a form boards can act on.

Professional Offices

Law, wealth management, and medical practices in downtown West Palm Beach hold sensitive client information and see a steady flow of visitors. A scored audit of access control, visitor management, and document handling gives partners a defensible baseline and a prioritized fix list.

Estates and Family Offices

The private client work described above extends across the county, from the Town of Palm Beach to Boca Raton: residential assessments, estate security planning, and family office programs handled under confidentiality agreements when requested.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about security consulting in West Palm Beach and Palm Beach County

Do you provide executive protection in Palm Beach?

As consultants, yes; as a staffing agency, no. Red Cell Solutions designs executive protection programs for Palm Beach principals, writes the standards a protective staffing vendor must meet, evaluates candidate firms, and helps you oversee the vendor you select. We do not supply bodyguards or armed agents, which keeps our advice independent of any payroll.

Does Red Cell Solutions travel to West Palm Beach?

Yes. The firm is based in Coral Springs, in neighboring Broward County, and performs on-site work throughout Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach, the Town of Palm Beach, and Boca Raton. There is no local branch office; assessments are conducted in person from the single South Florida location.

What does an estate security assessment in Palm Beach County cover?

An estate security assessment covers the property from the street inward: perimeter and landscaping sightlines, vehicle and pedestrian entries, camera and alarm coverage, lighting, and the procedures followed by family members and household staff. Findings are ranked by risk in a written estate security plan, and engagements are handled with strict discretion.

Do you work with houses of worship in Palm Beach County?

Yes. We help Palm Beach County congregations protect services, religious schools, and childcare areas while staying welcoming: greeter and usher protocols, access control for weekday operations, and emergency procedures designed for organizations that run largely on volunteers.

Do you assess private schools in Palm Beach County?

Yes. School assessments cover campus perimeter and entry control, visitor screening, dismissal and carline procedures, and staff readiness, with findings written so administrators and boards can act on them in priority order.
